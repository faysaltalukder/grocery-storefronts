## Licensing, attribution and privacy

- Each linked Pexels photo page displayed the **Free to use** designation when checked. Pexels' current license terms govern use; re-check before production promotion.
- Credits are displayed alongside images in the preview for provenance.
- These are stock photographs for visual demonstration only. They do not depict the actual provider, facilities, staff or patients.
- Do not use stock people as named doctors or actual employees; do not attach fabricated credentials, ratings, testimonials or patient stories.
- Images are currently loaded from Pexels' image CDN for this staging demonstration. Before production promotion, verify all image requests and consider downloading/optimizing them into the project's static asset storage or an approved image CDN to reduce third-party dependency and improve caching.
- Provide descriptive alt text, responsive crops and lazy loading below the fold. The hero image is eager-loaded.
- If an asset is removed or its license changes, replace it with another unique image and update this register.
