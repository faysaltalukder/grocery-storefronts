## Known QA boundary

The automated checks and database inventory were verified. A complete authenticated browser walkthrough of every page and a live enquiry submission were not performed in this release session. Preview mode intentionally blocks real enquiry submission; production operators must supply verified institutional content, contacts, policies and operational handling before publishing a public intake form.
