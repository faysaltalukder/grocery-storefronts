# Nonprofit & Organization — Four Template Implementation

**Standard:** MASTER_WEBSITE_ENGINEERING_STANDARD v2.1  
**Repository:** faysaltalukder/grocery-storefronts  
**Category:** `nonprofit-organization`  
**Release posture:** read-only showcase; payments and automated fulfilment disabled.

| Template | Business model | Visual identity | Main journey |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kindred Relief (`kindred-relief`) | Charity & Fundraising | #26384A / #C65D45 / #FAF4EC; Cormorant Garamond + DM Sans | Campaign discovery → giving information → pledge enquiry → staff review → donor follow-up. No payment is processed by this template. |
| Meridian Impact (`meridian-impact`) | NGO & Development Organization | #18384B / #D39B48 / #F4F6F2; Manrope + Inter | Programme discovery → evidence and partner review → institutional partnership enquiry → due diligence → staff-led follow-up. No grant or partnership is automatically awarded. |
| Wildroot Collective (`wildroot-collective`) | Environmental & Advocacy Organization | #234538 / #9A9B62 / #F1EEE3; Fraunces + DM Sans | Campaign or petition discovery → supporter action enquiry → consent and staff review → verified campaign follow-up. Petition signatures are not represented as collected unless a real service is configured. |
| Civic Circle (`civic-circle`) | Membership & Professional Association | #392D38 / #A98258 / #F5F0E8; Playfair Display + Manrope | Membership discovery → application enquiry → eligibility review → staff-confirmed membership and payment instructions. No account or paid membership is created automatically. |

Each template has 40 distinct page types and 83 unique first-party SVG illustrations (5 on home, 2 on each of 39 inner page types). Content is illustrative and must be replaced with verified operator information. Donation, membership and volunteer forms are operator-reviewed requests only. No card data is collected, no payment is processed, no recurring billing or member account is created, and no petition signature or impact number is fabricated. Website data uses existing website-scoped records and central administration.
