## Sample-content status

The eight launch-story concepts per template are illustrative editorial samples, not current news or verified reporting. Publishers must replace or fact-check sample copy, authorship, publication dates, source notes and claims before going live. Newsletter, news-tip and contact forms are preview-only and do not submit data.
