## Runtime image policy

- All 16 downloaded photographs are local first-party assets; no Pexels hotlinks are used by the storefront.
- The 40-page page catalog allocates 5 unique image records to the homepage and 2 unique, page-specific original SVG illustrations to each of the 39 inner pages (83 unique image records per template).
- Original SVG illustrations are generated locally by `newsMediaArtSrc()` and do not call an external image service.
- Photo contributor names are retained for provenance even though attribution is not required by the Pexels License.
